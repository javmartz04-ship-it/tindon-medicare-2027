IV · Side by side

How each path works.

Path 1

Original Medicare alone

  • Medicare pays about 80%
  • You pay 20%, with no yearly cap
  • No drug coverage built in
  • No dental, vision or hearing
Path 2

Original + a Supplement

  • Higher premium, predictable bills
  • Plan G: after the Part B deductible, no more medical bills
  • Plan N: after the deductible, small copays at the doctor
  • Any doctor in the U.S. who takes Medicare
  • No prior authorization
  • Add a separate drug plan
Path 3

Medicare Advantage

  • Often a $0 plan premium
  • Copays as you go, up to a yearly max
  • Drug coverage usually built in
  • Dental, vision and hearing included
  • Network and prior authorization
IV · Your options

Which path fits you?

Original + Supplement

A Supplement may fit if you…

  • Want predictable bills, even with a higher premium
  • See specialists or travel often
  • Want any doctor who takes Medicare
  • Don't want networks or prior approvals
Medicare Advantage

Advantage may fit if you…

  • Want a low or $0 monthly premium
  • Are comfortable using a network
  • Want drugs, dental, vision and hearing in one plan
  • Can handle copays up to the yearly max
An Advantage plan should never go alone. Hospital and critical illness coverage can help protect you from that out-of-pocket max.
